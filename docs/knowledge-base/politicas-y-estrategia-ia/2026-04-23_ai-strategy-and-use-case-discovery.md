## Slide 4

- Viamericas AI Strategy

- Artificial Intelligence will be embedded into how Viamericas operates to strengthen the agent network, increase customer engagement, improve corridor economics, and manage risk at scale. AI is not a separate initiative. It will support better decision-making across pricing, routing, credit, customer engagement, compliance, and cybersecurity, while improving operational efficiency across the organization.
- In addition, AI will enhance data-driven decision-making to improve the experience of both agents and senders, strengthening engagement, retention, and overall customer lifetime value.
- The goal is to improve revenue per agent, increase customer lifetime value, and reduce cost per transaction—while maintaining strong control over credit risk, fraud, compliance, and capital.

## Slide 6

- Viamericas AI Strategy

- Role of AI in the Business
- AI will be applied across the agent network, customer lifecycle, and transaction infrastructure to improve how decisions are made and executed. This includes enhancing how agents interact with senders, how customers engage with products, and how transactions are priced, routed, and monitored. It also strengthens how the company manages credit and market exposure, fraud risk, and compliance obligations. The focus is not on adopting AI for its own sake, but on solving practical business problems—improving growth, efficiency, and risk management in a measurable and scalable way.

## Slide 7

- Viamericas AI Strategy

- AI Strategic Initiatives Portfolio Management and Prioritization
- AI strategic initiatives will be managed as a coordinated portfolio to ensure focus, alignment, and efficient use of resources. Priority will be given to initiatives that offer high business impact with achievable implementation and clear paths to measurable results. Business impact will be evaluated based on expected improvements in revenue, margin, customer engagement, or risk reduction (including credit, fraud, and compliance).
- Each initiative will have a clearly defined business owner and specific objectives, and will be evaluated based on impact, feasibility, compliance and cybersecurity risks, and time to value. Business impact will be calculated by the Value Assurance team within the Data & A.I. area, ensuring measurable and financially grounded outcomes.
- The Head of AI will maintain the AI project portfolio, working with business and technology leadership to ensure clear prioritization, visibility, and alignment, while avoiding duplication and low-impact efforts. The prioritization of the AI initiatives portfolio will be conducted through the AI Committee, whose members are: the Chairman, CEO, EVP, CTO, CISO, and other senior managers as needed. The committee will meet monthly.

## Slide 8

- Viamericas AI Strategy

- Talent & Capability Development
- AI capabilities must be developed across the organization and cannot be limited to a single team. HR will play a key role as a partner to business and technology leadership in building this capability. This includes identifying skill gaps, supporting recruitment for key AI, data, and engineering roles, and developing targeted training programs.
- All AI training and capability development programs must be aligned with the policies defined by the CISO and the Head of Data & A.I., ensuring secure, responsible, and effective use of AI tools.
- Engineering teams will focus on applied AI skills, while business teams will focus on identifying opportunities and driving adoption. This is an ongoing effort to ensure the organization has the talent and skills needed as AI capabilities continue to evolve.

## Slide 10

- AI Opportunity Discovery Questions for Department Heads

- Data & Artificial Intelligence

- What is your more intensive repetitive processes?
- What would you do if you have unlimited time? Higher level thinking.
- Understanding current Workflows:
  - What are the most time-consuming processes your team performs regularly?
  - Which tasks are repetitive, rule-based, or require manual data entry?
  - Where do employees spend time that does not directly add value?
  - What processes rely heavily on spreadsheets, emails, or copy/paste work?
- Identifying Pain Points:
  - Where do errors or rework occur most frequently?
  - Where do employees express frustration or inefficiency?
  - What processes require multiple approvals or handoffs?
- Data & Reporting
  - What reports are created manually? How often?
  - How long does it take to prepare key reports or dashboards?
  - What data do you wish you had more quickly or accurately?
  - Are there decisions being made without real-time data?
