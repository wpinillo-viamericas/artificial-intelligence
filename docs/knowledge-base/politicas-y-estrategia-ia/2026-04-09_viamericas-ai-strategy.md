## Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence will be embedded into how Viamericas operates to strengthen the agent network, increase customer engagement, improve corridor economics, and manage risk at scale. AI is not a separate initiative. It will support better decision-making across pricing, routing, credit, customer engagement, compliance, and cybersecurity, while improving operational efficiency across the organization.

In addition, AI will enhance data-driven decision-making to improve the experience of both agents and senders, strengthening engagement, retention, and overall customer lifetime value.

The goal is to improve revenue per agent, increase customer lifetime value, and reduce cost per transaction—while maintaining strong control over credit risk, fraud, compliance, and capital.

## Core AI Capability Areas

- Agent Intelligence & Network Optimization: AI will provide agents with better visibility into sender behavior and performance. This includes identifying high value senders, recommending next actions, and improving how agents engage and retain them. The objective is to increase productivity and revenue per agent while strengthening the agent–sender relationship.

- Customer Intelligence & Engagement: AI will enable more personalized and continuous interaction with customers (agencies and senders). This includes segmentation, targeted messaging, and predictive insights to increase transaction frequency and retention. The goal is to move from one-time transactions to ongoing engagement across channels.

- Pricing, Routing & Corridor Optimization: AI will improve how transactions are priced and routed across corridors. This includes dynamic pricing, smarter partner selection, and better visibility into profitability. Even small improvements in these areas can drive meaningful margin expansion and cost efficiency.

- Credit, Market, Liquidity & Network Risk Intelligence: AI will strengthen how the company manages credit exposure, liquidity, and overall financial risk across its network. This includes agent risk scoring, liquidity forecasting, real-time monitoring of exposure, and market risk management. Special emphasis will be placed on managing foreign exchange (FX) exposure, including monitoring and optimizing the company’s position in foreign currencies and mitigating the impact of exchange rate fluctuations on financial performance.

- Compliance, Fraud & Trust Intelligence: AI will enhance fraud detection, AML processes, and regulatory reporting. By identifying patterns and anomalies more effectively, AI can reduce false positives, improve detection rates, and strengthen trust with regulators and partners.

- Cybersecurity: AI will enhance cybersecurity detection and response capabilities by being a force multiplier. Agents can be used to investigate, triage, and manage alerts, reducing investigation and remediation time, AI can also help detect false positives, cutting through noise and allowing cybersecurity personnel to focus on risks that have the potential to impact Viamericas’ operations.

- AI based software development can be used to create custom internal tooling that makes processes more effective. Something that in the past couldn’t be achieved without significant investment in security products.

- Operations & Automation: AI and automation will reduce manual work across operations, including reconciliation, document processing, and internal workflows. This will improve speed, reduce cost, and allow teams to focus on higher-value activities.

- Knowledge Management & System Intelligence: AI will be used to document, structure, and make accessible Viamericas’ existing systems, processes, and data flows. This includes leveraging generative AI to automatically generate and maintain technical and functional documentation, enabling greater transparency and reducing dependency on individual knowledge.

- These capabilities will support faster onboarding, improve cross-team collaboration, and enhance decision-making by providing easy access to institutional knowledge. Over time, this will evolve into an AI-powered knowledge layer that allows teams to interact with internal systems and documentation in a more intuitive and efficient way.

## Role of AI in the Business

AI will be applied across the agent network, customer lifecycle, and transaction infrastructure to improve how decisions are made and executed. This includes enhancing how agents interact with senders, how customers engage with products, and how transactions are priced, routed, and monitored. It also strengthens how the company manages credit and market exposure, fraud risk, and compliance obligations. The focus is not on adopting AI for its own sake, but on solving practical business problems—improving growth, efficiency, and risk management in a measurable and scalable way.

## Leadership and Implementation Model

AI will be delivered through a lean, embedded model that reflects how Viamericas operates—focused on clear ownership, minimal overhead, and direct collaboration between business, technology, and risk. The Head of Data & AI leads execution and is responsible for the AI project portfolio and delivery of outcomes. This role is also accountable for ensuring that all AI solutions are built on governed and trusted data, minimizing hallucinations and inconsistent outputs. The CISO leads governance, ensuring that AI is secure, compliant, and aligned with regulatory expectations. The CTO works closely with both to ensure that AI capabilities are integrated into core systems and scalable across the organization. Business unit heads are directly involved and accountable for outcomes within their areas. AI is not delivered through a separate function—business, AI, and technology work together from the start, ensuring solutions are practical, adopted quickly, and embedded into operations.

## AI Delivery Structure

AI at Viamericas is delivered through a structured but flexible approach that balances focused investment with broad adoption across the business. There are two complementary modes of delivery.

AI Solutions Classification Framework

To ensure a structured and scalable adoption of Artificial Intelligence across Viamericas, AI solutions can be categorized into four main groups:

1. Predictive Analytics (Advanced Analytics)

These solutions use historical and real-time data to identify patterns, predict future outcomes, and support decision-making.

Typical applications include pricing optimization, credit risk modeling, customer behavior prediction, and demand forecasting.

Their primary value lies in improving decision quality, increasing revenue, and reducing risk.

2. Process Automation (RPA + AI)

These solutions combine robotic process automation with AI capabilities to automate repetitive, rule-based, and semi-structured tasks.

Examples include onboarding processes (KYC), reconciliation, document processing, and operational workflows.

Their main benefit is increased efficiency, reduced operational costs, and lower error rates.

3. Generative AI

Generative AI solutions leverage large language models and other advanced techniques to create content, generate insights, and assist decision-making.

Use cases include sales copilots, customer interaction support, document generation, and knowledge assistants.

These solutions significantly enhance productivity, enable better customer engagement, and support faster decision cycles.

4. AI-Enabled Development and Intelligent Platforms

These solutions focus on embedding AI into software development and enterprise platforms. This includes AI-assisted coding, intelligent data platforms, and systems that integrate AI into core business processes.

They enable scalability, accelerate development cycles, and ensure that AI capabilities are embedded directly into business operations rather than deployed as isolated tools.

Together, these four categories provide a comprehensive framework to guide AI adoption, ensuring that Viamericas balances revenue growth, operational efficiency, and risk management while building sustainable internal capabilities.

Before adopting any AI tool, it must be reviewed and approved by the Head of Data & A.I., the CISO, and the CTO. Approved tools must be included in:

- The AI tools inventory managed by the CISO

- The AI solutions inventory managed by the Head of Data & A.I.

This ensures security, compliance, and alignment with enterprise data and technology standards.

There are two complementary modes of delivery:

- The first is focused strategic initiatives, which target a small number of high-impact areas such as pricing, credit risk, fraud, customer engagement, and market risk. Also, these initiatives have clear business ownership, defined KPIs, and dedicated support from AI and technology resources. Work is delivered in short cycles, typically starting with pilots to validate impact before scaling into production. All initiatives must be aligned with the company’s business strategy and integrated into the OKRs defined by the respective business areas.

- The second is embedded AI within everyday development. Engineering teams are expected to identify where AI can improve the features, workflows, and systems they are already building. Before implementation, engineering teams must confirm with the Data & A.I. team that all inputs used by AI solutions are based on governed data or official data sources, and with the CISO to analyze use cases, technologies, and data involved, against applicable regulations, privacy implications, and the potential of such tools to introduce vulnerabilities in Viamericas technology stack. This work does not require separate project structures or approvals—AI becomes part of how products are developed. This approach ensures that Viamericas can pursue major opportunities while continuously improving the business across all areas.

## AI Strategic Initiatives Portfolio Management and Prioritization

AI strategic initiatives will be managed as a coordinated portfolio to ensure focus, alignment, and efficient use of resources. Priority will be given to initiatives that offer high business impact with achievable implementation and clear paths to measurable results. Business impact will be evaluated based on expected improvements in revenue, margin, customer engagement, or risk reduction (including credit, fraud, and compliance).

Each initiative will have a clearly defined business owner and specific objectives, and will be evaluated based on impact, feasibility, compliance and cybersecurity risks, and time to value. Business impact will be calculated by the Value Assurance team within the Data & A.I. area, ensuring measurable and financially grounded outcomes.

The Head of AI will maintain the AI project portfolio, working with business and technology leadership to ensure clear prioritization, visibility, and alignment, while avoiding duplication and low-impact efforts. The prioritization of the AI initiatives portfolio will be conducted through the AI Committee, whose members are: the Chairman, CEO, EVP, CTO, CISO, and other senior managers as needed. The committee will meet monthly.

## AI Consideration in Routine Development

AI should be considered as part of all routine technology development. For any new feature, enhancement, or process change, teams should evaluate whether AI can improve outcomes, including automation, better decision-making, or enhanced user experience. Engineering teams, working with business stakeholders and the Value Assurance team from Data & A.I., are responsible for identifying opportunities where AI can generate material value. This evaluation is part of standard development and does not require separate approval. Engineering teams, working with business stakeholders, are responsible for identifying and incorporating AI where it adds value.

## AI-Enabled Development

Before using AI tools, engineering teams must obtain approval from both the CISO and the Head of Data & A.I. to ensure:

- No leakage of internal, confidential, or restricted information

- Compatibility with governed data sources and/or the enterprise Data Lakehouse

AI-assisted software development will be a key driver of efficiency across the technology organization. Engineering teams are expected to use AI tools for code generation, testing, debugging, and documentation as part of their daily workflows. This is not a separate initiative, but an evolution in how software is built. While initial training has begun, additional hands-on training will be provided to strengthen practical capability and ensure consistent adoption across teams. The objective is to increase development speed, reduce repetitive work, and improve overall productivity. AI tools will also support automatic and continuous documentation of systems and processes, ensuring knowledge is consistently captured and accessible across the organization.

## Talent & Capability Development

AI capabilities must be developed across the organization and cannot be limited to a single team. HR will play a key role as a partner to business and technology leadership in building this capability. This includes identifying skill gaps, supporting recruitment for key AI, data, and engineering roles, and developing targeted training programs.

All AI training and capability development programs must be aligned with the policies defined by the CISO and the Head of Data & A.I., ensuring secure, responsible, and effective use of AI tools.

Engineering teams will focus on applied AI skills, while business teams will focus on identifying opportunities and driving adoption. This is an ongoing effort to ensure the organization has the talent and skills needed as AI capabilities continue to evolve.

## Foundational Requirements

AI success at Viamericas depends on strong data, governance, and technology foundations. These are not standalone initiatives, but core capabilities that enable all AI use cases and ensure they deliver reliable, scalable, and compliant results. These foundational efforts are already underway and will continue to evolve in parallel with AI use cases. Strengthening these areas ensures that AI initiatives can move from pilot to production effectively and deliver sustained business impact.

- Data Foundation: A unified and reliable data environment is critical. This includes integrating sender, agent, transaction, and partner data into a consistent and accessible structure, improving data quality, and enabling real-time or near real-time data availability where needed. Current efforts are focused on making data usable for priority use cases, with an emphasis on practicality and incremental improvement rather than large-scale redesign.

- Technology Platform and Integration: Scalable technology platforms are required to build, deploy, and integrate AI capabilities into core systems and workflows. This includes ensuring that AI solutions can be embedded into transaction systems, agent tools, customer-facing applications, and internal processes. The focus is on enabling practical deployment and avoiding isolated or experimental solutions that cannot scale.

- The CTO is responsible for ensuring that AI capabilities are aligned with the overall technology architecture and can be integrated into core systems in a scalable and reliable way. This includes supporting the infrastructure, standards, and integration needed to move AI solutions from pilot to production.

- Governance: AI must be implemented in a way that is secure, compliant, and transparent. Governance establishes the policies, standards, and controls that guide how AI is developed, deployed, and used across the organization. This governance is shared across key roles, with clear accountability:

- Model governance (Head of AI)

- The Head of AI is responsible for ensuring models are properly designed, validated, monitored, and periodically reviewed for performance, accuracy, and explainability. This includes ensuring models remain fit for purpose as data and business conditions evolve.

- Risk and compliance (Chief Compliance Officer)

- The Chief Compliance Officer is responsible for ensuring that AI use cases meet regulatory requirements and that credit risk, fraud, and compliance considerations are incorporated into design and deployment. This includes oversight of how AI impacts regulatory reporting and decision-making.

- Data usage, privacy, and security (CISO)

- The CISO is responsible for data protection, security, and appropriate use of data in AI systems. This includes defining and enforcing data usage policies, managing access controls, and ensuring that AI systems meet cybersecurity and privacy standards.

## Quarterly Reporting and Review

Quarterly reporting will be conducted through the AI Committee, ensuring visibility and alignment at the highest level of the organization. That report will be led by the Head of AI and prepared in coordination with the CTO, CISO, and Head of HR. The report will include efficiency gains delivered, AI project portfolio and prioritization; status of initiatives in progress; AI training and capability development (engineering and non-engineering); data foundation progress; security, credit risk, fraud, and compliance updates; and key blockers, risks, and dependencies.

## AI Strategy Continuous Review

This review will ensure that the AI strategy continues to support the overall business strategy of Viamericas and will be presented for approval to the AI Committee. AI is a rapidly evolving area, and this strategy will be reviewed regularly to remain effective. At a minimum, it will be reviewed annually by the Head of AI, CTO, and CISO, with input from business leaders. The review will incorporate new technology developments, lessons learned from implementation, and alignment with business priorities.

## Measuring Success

The measurement of value generated by AI initiatives will be led by the Value Assurance team within the Data & A.I. area, ensuring that all outcomes are quantified in terms of financial and operational impact. Success will be measured through improvements in revenue per agent, customer engagement and retention, cost efficiency and cost per transaction, reduction in credit losses, fraud, and compliance risk, and adoption of AI across business and technology teams.
